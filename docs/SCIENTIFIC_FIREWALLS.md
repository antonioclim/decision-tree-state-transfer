# Scientific interpretation firewalls

- Study 1 and Study 2 are not pooled.
- H₂ concerns practical equivalence of complete policies, not universal irrelevance of diversity.
- H₃ concerns selected retained-content utility relative to the specified replacement/sham, not generic ancestry.
- J₁ and J₂ are order-specific increments, not Shapley or order-independent component values.
- Jₛᵤₘ is derived and has no new confirmatory p-value.
- Resource coordinates remain in native units and do not establish monetary cost-effectiveness.
- Peak RSS is a shared-process lifetime high-water mark and is excluded from arm-specific Pareto claims.
- The synthetic scenario mixture does not establish unrestricted deployment generality.
