# F13 figure captions and table notes

## Figure 1 — Primary confirmatory effects
Point estimates and prospectively frozen simultaneous 98.33% bootstrap-t intervals for H1–H3. Positive contrasts favour retaining fitted state or inherited material. The shaded region marks the prespecified practical-equivalence band of ±0.5 percentage points absolute loss. H1 and H3 are classified BENEFICIAL; H2 is classified EQUIVALENT.

## Figure 2 — Secondary mechanism associations
Additive scenario-fixed-effect associations for eligible H3 checkpoints. Continuous predictors are standardised. Positive coefficients denote a larger replace-minus-sham loss contrast and hence a larger observed advantage of retaining the selected inherited material. Intervals are secondary 95% scenario-stream cluster-bootstrap intervals. The class-deficiency indicator is shown without an interval because bootstrap support was insufficient; these coefficients are associational and must not be described as causal mediation.

## Figure 3 — Contextual comparator loss–CPU frontier
Equal-scenario mean prequential loss versus process CPU time for the six runtime-qualified contextual methods on the frozen 280-stream subset. The connected line is the supported lower CPU–loss frontier under a non-negative linear CPU scalarisation. ARF is Pareto-nondominated pointwise but unsupported; EFDT and ROLLING_CART are dominated by HAT on mean loss and total CPU. Comparator results provide predictive/resource context only and do not identify H1–H3.

## Figure 4 — Internal intervention decision plane
The vertical axis is the future-loss penalty of the counterfactual alternative relative to retention; the horizontal axis is the additional realised process CPU of that alternative. H1 compares RESTART-CART with PERSIST, H2 compares CHAMPION-RESEED with PERSIST and H3 compares MATERIAL-REPLACE with STRUCTURAL-SHAM. The shaded band is the prespecified ±0.5 percentage-point practical-equivalence region. APW is deliberately not plotted on this realised-CPU axis.

## Figure S1 — Scenario-level descriptive contrasts
Scenario-specific component means for H1, H2 and the conditional H3 ratio. These are descriptive heterogeneity diagnostics, not separate confirmatory tests and have no scenario-specific multiplicity-adjusted intervals. LT denotes LOCAL_TREE and OB denotes OBLIQUE.

## Figure S2 — H3 eligibility
Prospectively defined material-eligibility rates at both checkpoints for all 14 scenarios. Percentages are printed in every cell so interpretation is not colour-dependent.

## Table notes
Table 1 contains the confirmatory F11 family and must retain its simultaneous intervals and Holm-adjusted p-values. Table 2 separates APW from realised CPU/elapsed/RSS metrics. Table 3 contains contextual comparator evidence only. Supplementary tables contain horizon sensitivity, mechanism coefficients and scenario-level descriptives. No table licenses monetary cost-effectiveness claims.
