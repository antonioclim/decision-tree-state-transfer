# Licence decision matrix

| Route | Software | Protocols and generated data | Advantages | Risks / required authority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A — retain restrictive multi-licence (recommended provisional route)** | PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 | CC BY-NC 4.0 | Matches the existing private-repository policy and protects commercial rights | All applicable rights holders must confirm the policy before release |
| B — permissive research software | Apache-2.0 or BSD-3-Clause | CC BY 4.0 | Maximises reuse and interoperability | Requires explicit relicensing authority from every rights holder; not inferred here |
| C — code visible, no grant of reuse | All rights reserved | All rights reserved | Lowest legal overreach if rights are uncertain | Weak reproducibility and reuse; generally unsuitable for a companion repository |

The candidate is constructed under Route A but remains non-public until rights are explicitly confirmed.
